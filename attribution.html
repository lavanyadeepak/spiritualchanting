About SSRF Recordings
Several chants (in the ./chants/ssrf/ folder) originate from the Spiritual Science Research Foundation (SSRF) — a spiritual organisation that produces and distributes chanting audio. Their recordings are generally made available for personal spiritual use. However, redistribution, embedding in apps, or commercial use may require explicit permission from SSRF. It is recommended to:

1. Contact SSRF at ssrf.org to confirm redistribution rights.
2. Credit recordings clearly if permission is granted.
3. Replace with self-recorded or Creative Commons alternatives if permission is denied.
General Usage Guidelines
For personal/private use: All recordings used solely for personal spiritual practice are generally permissible under fair use principles in most jurisdictions.

For open-source distribution (GitHub): The app engine code is open source. Audio files are excluded from the repository (as noted in the README) — this is the correct approach. Do not commit audio files without explicit copyright clearance.

For the Docker image (Byte Shrine): If audio files are bundled in the Docker image, ensure each recording is either self-recorded, licensed under Creative Commons, or written permission has been obtained from the copyright holder.

The sacred texts themselves (Sanskrit mantras, Tamil stotras, Vedic hymns) are ancient works in the global public domain and may be freely used, recited, and shared.
